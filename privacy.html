Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. Local Storage & Profile Customization

NullDay operates entirely on your device as a local-first application. All daily Win/Loss accountability logs, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device using Capacitor Preferences. None of this data is transmitted to or stored on any external server, cloud service, or third party operated by NullDay.

You may optionally customize your profile by entering a Name and selecting a Profile Photo inside the app settings. This profile data is compressed and stored exclusively as local device preferences, remains under your sole control, and is never uploaded to our servers.

2. No Cloud Account Required

NullDay does not require sign-up, email registration, phone authentication, or cloud account creation. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal identity details, name, or profile photos to our servers.

3. Google AdMob Integration

To keep NullDay free, we display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data such as Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, network type, and basic device identifiers to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads in line with applicable laws including GDPR and CCPA. Consent is requested where legally required.

  • You can withdraw or reset ad consent choices any time using the consent form settings button inside the App.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's system settings under "Privacy -> Ads".
  • Read Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. App Permissions

NullDay requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Internet / Access Network State — required for ad delivery.
  • Post Notifications — for optional daily check-in reminders, only if you explicitly enable it.

5. Data Deletion & Rectification

You have full control over your local data:

  • Rectification: You can edit today's accountability log directly on the App's home page.
  • Deletion: You can permanently erase all your logs, streak records, name, and profile photo instantly by clicking the "Delete All Local Data" button inside the App Settings. Alternatively, clearing App storage/cache in your Android system settings or uninstalling the App will permanently delete all records.

6. India DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

For our users in India, we adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. User data remains on your physical device. To register any grievance, contact our Grievance Officer:

  • Grievance Officer: Jaimin Prajapati
  • Email: jaiminadac@gmail.com
  • Log Retention: Any automated temporary logs captured by third-party advertising SDK endpoints are stored for the legally mandated period of 1 year.

7. Children's Privacy

NullDay is not directed to children under 13 (or under 18 in EEA/India). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will assist.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version.
