Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. Local Storage Architecture

NullDay operates entirely on your device. All accountability logs, streaks, goals, notes, tags, and progress data are stored locally using Capacitor Preferences. None of this data is transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party operated by NullDay.

2. No Account Required

NullDay does not require sign-up, email, phone number, or any personal account. We do not collect names, contact details, location, or device identifiers ourselves.

3. Google AdMob Integration

To keep NullDay free, we display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data such as advertising ID, IP address, network type, and basic device identifiers to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads in line with applicable laws including GDPR. Consent is requested where required.

  • You can reset ad consent any time from Settings → "Reset Ad Consent".
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's Privacy settings.
  • Read Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Permissions

NullDay requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet — required for ad delivery only.
  • Post Notifications — for optional daily reminder, only if you enable it in Settings.
  • Schedule Exact Alarm — for accurate reminder timing.

5. Data Export & Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Backup: Settings → Copy Backup JSON to export every record.
  • Import: Restore data from a previous backup file.
  • Delete: Settings → Clear All Data permanently removes everything from your device.

Since no data leaves your device (other than ad-network data handled by Google), uninstalling the app or clearing data fully satisfies any data deletion request.

6. Children's Privacy

NullDay is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data via the app, contact us and we will assist.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version. Material changes will also be communicated inside the app where reasonable.
