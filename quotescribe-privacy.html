Privacy Policy for QuoteScribe

Last updated: May 21, 2026

At QuoteScribe, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data we collect. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Credentials: When you sign in (via Google Sign-In or Email), we collect your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • User-Generated Content: We collect the quotes, positive thoughts, and experiences that you write, post, and share in the App.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Identifiers: We collect device-specific IDs (e.g., FCM Tokens) to deliver push notifications.
  • Ad Identifiers: We use Google AdMob, which collects advertising IDs for personalized ads.
  • Diagnostics and Performance Data: We collect crash logs, performance metrics, and application activity logs using Firebase Crashlytics to maintain App stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, run, and maintain the App.
  • Authenticate your account and enable you to publish and share quotes.
  • Send you notification alerts (which you can disable in settings).
  • Improve the performance, design, and stability of the App.
  • Show non-personalized or personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only in the following scenarios:

  • With Service Providers: We use Google Firebase (Firestore, Auth, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) and Google AdMob to run the backend and ad services.
  • Public Content: Any quotes or positive thoughts you write and publish are visible to other users of the App.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

4. Data Security and Retention

  • Security: We use secure HTTPS/SSL protocols and Firebase security rules to encrypt and protect your data in transit and at rest.
  • Retention: We retain your personal information as long as you keep your account active. You can request account and data deletion at any time.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Account Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and all associated user-generated quotes by contacting us at jaiminadac@gmail.com.
  • Notifications: You can opt out of receiving push notifications by turning them off in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
